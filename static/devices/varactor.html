Equations and model

    

      
    
        
  • Junction capacitanceCJ = CJO / (1 − VD / VJ)MVD is negative in reverse bias, so the capacitance falls as the reverse voltage grows. M is about 0.5 for an abrupt junction, about 0.33 for a graded one and larger for a hyperabrupt tuning diode.
    • 
        
  • Parallel platesCJ = ε · A / WThe depletion region of width W is the insulating gap, and the neutral p and n regions are the plates of area A.
    • 
        
  • Tuned circuitf = 1 / (2π · √(L · CJ))With an inductor L across it, the varactor sets the resonant frequency, so a control voltage tunes the circuit.
    • 
        
  • Zero biasCJ = CJOThe largest capacitance in normal use.
    • 
        
  • Reverse biasCJ < CJOThe working region. Almost no current flows, so the diode behaves as a capacitor.
