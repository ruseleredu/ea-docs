Model: SPICE level 1 (square-law) enhancement MOSFET with the parameters above and W/L = 1. The body is tied to the source. No subthreshold current, no gate leakage and no breakdown. The p-channel version uses the same magnitudes with every voltage and current reversed. The channel and depletion shapes are simplified sketches with the channel thickness greatly exaggerated, and carrier speeds are compressed so that small and large currents are both visible. The readouts and curves use the equations.
