These pages are written from scratch in HTML5 canvas and plain JavaScript. They cover the same ground as the Flash animations Edward Hill made in 2002/2003 for the first-year Linear Circuits and Devices course at the Cambridge University Engineering Department, later revised by Michael Khaw, and add the Zener, Schottky, light-emitting and varactor diodes, the thyristor, the bipolar transistor, the MESFET and the IGBT.

Each explorer uses an idealised textbook model and states its parameters and simplifications at the bottom of its page.
