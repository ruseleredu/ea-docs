Model: SPICE diode at 300 K with a reverse-breakdown term (BV, IBV and NBV as in PSpice and LTspice) and no internal series resistance. The depletion width assumes heavy doping, about 1018 cm−3 on each side, and the split between tunnelling and avalanche is described from BV alone. The diode voltage is measured at the p side relative to the n side, so a negative supply reverse biases it. The number of carriers shown crossing is compressed so that both small and large currents are visible; the readouts and the curve use the real equations.
