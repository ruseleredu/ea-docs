Model: the SPICE3 n-channel MESFET (Statz) drain-current equations with the parameters above. No gate conduction, so the gate is limited to 0 V and below; no breakdown, no capacitances and no source or drain resistance. The depletion-region shape is a simplified sketch that assumes a 0.8 V Schottky barrier, and carrier speeds are compressed so that small and large currents are both visible. The readouts and curves use the equations.
