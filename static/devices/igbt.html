Equations and model

    

      
    
        
  • pnp sectionIC = (1 + BF) · IMOSThe electron current through the MOSFET channel is the base current of the pnp transistor. The collector current is that base current plus the hole current it calls up.

  • ActiveIMOS = (KP / 2) · (VGE − VTO)2 · (1 + LAMBDA · VDS)When VDS ≥ VGE − VTO. The channel is pinched off and the gate voltage sets the current.

Model: a SPICE level 1 n-channel MOSFET (VTO, KP, LAMBDA, W/L = 1) supplying the base current of a pnp transistor (IS, BF), which is the usual two-device macro-model of an IGBT. No tail current, no latch-up, no temperature effects and no breakdown, and the gain of the pnp section is taken as constant. The collector voltage range is kept low so that the on-state region is readable; a real IGBT blocks hundreds of volts. The cross-section is a sketch of one cell with exaggerated layer thicknesses, and carrier numbers and speeds are compressed. The readouts and curves use the equations.
